About

Student profile

This portfolio documents a 5-week teaching practicum with Class XII students, bridging the official board syllabus with Applied Linguistics and English Language Teaching principles.

The instructional design connects the Class XII English syllabus (Book II Prose, Heroes, Goodbye Mr. Chips, and Grammar/Composition) with Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL), Swales' Genre Analysis, Speech Act Theory, and Form-Meaning-Use grammar frameworks — to strengthen students' board exam preparation while building authentic academic and career readiness skills.

What I set out to do

  • Bridge the Class XII English syllabus (Book II Prose, Heroes, Goodbye Mr. Chips, Grammar/Composition) with Applied Linguistics and ELT principles
  • Integrate the English for Academic & Career Readiness (EACR) course into regular lessons
  • Apply SFL, Swales' Genre Analysis, Speech Act Theory and Form-Meaning-Use grammar in real lessons
  • Strengthen students' board exam preparation with targeted, exam-style practice
  • Develop authentic academic writing and career readiness skills (CVs, emails, interviews)

15-hour assessment framework

Exactly 15 hours of instruction (3 hours a week) went to formal and formative assessment.

  1. Week 1 · 3.0hDiagnostic Baseline (1h), Prose Reading Quiz (1h), Error-Correction Test (1h)
  2. Week 2 · 3.0hTenses Diagnostic (1h), Paragraph Writing Task (1h), Oral CLT Rubric Evaluation (1h)
  3. Week 3 · 3.0hVoice Transformation Quiz (1h), Mid-Term Progress Exam (1.5h), CV Draft Check (0.5h)
  4. Week 4 · 3.0hPreposition/Lexical Check (1h), Novel Analysis Test (1h), Mock Interview Rubric (1h)
  5. Week 5 · 3.0hReported Speech Test (1h), Final Cumulative Board Exam (1.5h), Portfolio Self-Assessment (0.5h)
